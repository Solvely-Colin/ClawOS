# Third-party and publication notes

ClawOS integrates Arch Linux tooling, OpenClaw, Sway, Chromium, Foot, systemd,
Plymouth, QEMU and the Carapace design language. The visual prototype uses React,
Vite and Radix icons. These projects retain their own licenses and ownership;
a future ClawOS license must not replace theirs.

This private baseline does not introduce a public license. Before publication,
review copied assets, icons, generated artwork, redistribution and upstream
patches, and record required attributions and license texts. Package manifests
are not a complete asset-provenance record.
